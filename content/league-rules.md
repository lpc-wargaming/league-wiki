# League Rules

> [!warning] Draft
> 11th Edition rules are still rolling out. Points values and mission references will be finalized once the core rulebook is in hand.

## Format

- **Phases:** 4 monthly phases — 500 → 750 → 1000 → 1500 points
- **Games:** Play as many league games as you like per phase; your best results count (see scoring)
- **Lists:** You may rebuild your list between phases, but units you've already fielded are encouraged to stay (this is your army growing, not a new army each month)
- **Rules:** 11th Edition matched play rules, missions from the current Chapter Approved deck

## Scoring

| Result | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Win | 3 |
| Draw | 2 |
| Loss | 1 |
| First game vs. a new opponent (each phase) | +1 |
| Army fully painted for the phase (3 colors + based) | +2 |
| Submit a short battle report or army photo for the wiki | +1 |

Maximum of **3 scored games per phase** — play more if you want, but nobody can grind the standings.

## Sportsmanship

This league exists to get people playing. Rules disputes: roll a d6, move on, look it up after the game. Slow-play, gotcha rules lawyering, and netlist-stomping new players are all worth less than being someone people want to play again.

## New Player Accommodations

- Phase 1 games can be played with unpainted/unassembled-but-built models, proxies allowed with opponent's consent
- League organizer or veterans will run demo/teaching games on request
- Crusade/narrative rules are **optional** — the default is simple matched play
