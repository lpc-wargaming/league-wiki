# Getting Started — Your First 500 Points

Never played 40k? Perfect. This league is built for you.

## Step 1: Pick a Faction

Pick the army that looks coolest to you. Seriously — you'll be painting and playing these models for months, so aesthetics matter more than rules. Browse factions at [warhammer.com](https://www.warhammer.com) or ask anyone at the store.

The **Armageddon launch box** (Orks vs Space Marines) is the best value if either faction appeals to you — each half is roughly a league-ready starting force, and you can often split the box with another player.

## Step 2: Get to 500 Points

Most factions have a **Combat Patrol box** that gets you most of the way to 500 points in one purchase. See [[resources|the buyer's guides]] for per-faction recommendations.

You do **not** need:
- A painted army (this league is to encourage painting your army, we won't shame you for being behind!)
- The big rulebook (core rules are free as a PDF from Games Workshop)
- To know the rules (we'll teach you in your first game)

## Step 3: Build a List

Free official app: the 40k app handles list-building. Ask the organizer or any veteran to check your first list — it takes five minutes. Alternative apps will catch up soon.

## Step 4: Play

Show up on league nights, find an opponent, play. Your first game can be a teaching game with a veteran — it won't count against your standings unless you want it to.

## Common Questions

**What does 500 points cost?** Typically one Combat Patrol box (~$170) covers it, often with room to grow into phase 2. 3d printed models ok, but support your FLGS whenever you can!

**What else do I need?** Dice, a tape measure, glue, clippers. The store stocks all of it.

**What if I lose every game?** You still score league points every game, painting scores points, and everyone's first league is a losing league. The goal is to trick yourself into having a painted army at the end.
