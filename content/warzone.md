# The Warzone

> [!warning] Working draft
> This is the base concept, to be fleshed out in a refining session. The mechanics below are a starting point, not final.

> [!note] What this is
> The Warzone is the league's **narrative campaign** — a season-long fight over a map of the setting. Every league game is also a battle here. It runs *alongside* the [[league-rules|league]]: the league rewards **you** (league points and standing), the Warzone rewards your **faction** (territory, and the story). It's the same reported result feeding two systems, so it adds no rules to your games.

> The Warzone is one *kind* of campaign. If it endures, we can run other narrative campaigns under the same roof later — this is just the first.

## The Map

The contested region is divided into territories, tracked on [[lore|the setting wiki]]. *(Map goes here — an image, an Obsidian Canvas, or a simple list of zones.)* Each territory is worth **1 control point**; a few key sites are worth more and are marked on the map.

Every player flies a banner — your army's faction. *(If the factions are lopsided, we'll group everyone into two or three **powers** so the war has clear sides.)*

## Fighting for Territory

When you report a league game, name the **territory** you fought over — usually one bordering your faction's holdings (push the front line) or any neutral or contested zone.

- **Win** → your faction takes or holds that territory.
- **Draw** → the territory stays as it was, or becomes contested if it was neutral.

The organizer updates the map after each league night and writes a line of story for the wiki as the front shifts.

## Winning the War

At the finale, the faction (or power) holding the most control points wins **the Warzone** — a collective narrative victory, separate from the League Champion. **The final map becomes canon** in the setting lore, so the season you play writes the history everyone references next time.

## Joining Mid-War

Show up whenever. Your faction arrives as **reinforcements** in a neutral or friendly border zone and fights from there. The story expands to fit you — that's the whole catch-up mechanic.

## Optional Flavour

All skippable, and **none are locked in** — these are candidates to work through in the refining session:

- **Entrench** — a *fully painted* army that takes a territory digs in. To dislodge it, an attacker must beat it there twice. (Painting helps you hold ground — another nudge toward the brush.)
- **Theatre objectives** — a seasonal goal, e.g. "hold the capital by the finale," that unlocks a lore reveal when met.
- **Named survivors** — a painted character model that lives through the season earns a name and an entry in [[lore|the setting wiki]].

## Reporting

Report Warzone results the same way you report league games — to the organizer or in [DISCORD/CHANNEL]. One report covers both.
