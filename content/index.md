## What is an Escalation League?

You start with a small army (500 points) and grow it each month. Play games at your own pace, paint as you go, and by the end of the league you'll have a full 1500-point painted army — and a bunch of new opponents who started exactly where you did.

**No experience required. No painted models required to start. Just show up.**
