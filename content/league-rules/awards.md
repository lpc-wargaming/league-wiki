# Awards

Everything here sits beside the **League Champion**, which is won on points over in the [[content/league-rules/index#scoring|rules]]. One award you earn just by painting; the rest are handed out at the finale.

## Painting Stickers — Earned Every Phase

Each phase, paint your army to that phase's size (3 colours + based) and you earn a **sticker** on your league card. It's the same standard as the +2 painting bonus in the rules, so one finished army pays twice — points *and* a sticker.

Collect a sticker every phase and at the finale you take home the **Iron Hobbyist** pin/trophy: the badge for painting the whole way through. No vote, no tie, no debate — you either painted it or you didn't.

## Finale Awards

Given out once, on finale night. Kept short on purpose, so the votes don't scatter and each award means something different:

| Award | Decided by | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **League Champion** | Most points — see [[content/league-rules/index#tiebreakers\|tiebreakers]] | Winning the league |
| **Best Painted** | Player vote | An army at its peak |
| **Most Improved** | Player vote | How far your brush travelled |
| **Best Sport** | Player vote | Being the opponent people want to face again |
| **Wooden Spoon** | Last in the final standings | Showing up anyway — worn with pride |
| **Iron Hobbyist** | Painted every phase (the sticker capstone) | Going the distance |

Each comes with a pin. **Best Painted** and **Most Improved** reward different things on purpose — one is the best army *now*, the other is the biggest leap from where you started — so a newcomer can take home glory standing right next to a veteran.

## Voting

- Ballots are **pre-printed with every player's name and army**, and your own is struck off your sheet — no self-voting.
- Each category takes a **ranked pick** (1st, 2nd, 3rd choice). If the first-choice votes tie, the lower choices break it, so the night ends with a single winner per award and no run-offs.
- The **store owner or an employee tallies the ballots** — a neutral hand, and no player sees the count.

> The Wooden Spoon is the one place a co-winner is fine — a shared spoon is funnier than a lonely one. Everything else resolves to one winner.
