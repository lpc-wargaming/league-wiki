# Awards

Everything here sits beside the **League Champion**, which is won on points over in the [[content/league-rules/index#scoring|rules]]. The awards split three ways: one you build up phase by phase just by painting, three the players vote on at the finale, and two that fall out of the final standings.

## The Iron Hobbyist

Each phase, paint your army to that phase's size. If you have a fully painted army at the end of the league, you get a special award.

> **Iron Hobbyist** : The crowning achievement for completing the league. You did it, you went the distance and have a fully painted army. Game performance is irrelevant. This is the one that matters.

If you join late or just fall behind, no worries. If you hit it hard at the end, you can bounce back and still earn the award.

## Finale Awards

Handed out on finale night. The Iron Hobbyist and your painted army may be the ultimate goal, but it doesn't mean we can't have some fun with some other awards.

### Player's Choice

These awards are voted on by your peers.

| Award             | Rewards                          |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Best Painted**  | Made your army men a work of art |
| **Most Improved** | How far your brush travelled     |
| **Remembrancer**  | Brought your army to life        |

**Best Painted** and **Most Improved** reward different things on purpose. One is the best army *now*, the other is the biggest leap from where you started. This means a newcomer can take home glory standing right next to a veteran. **Remembrancer** goes to whoever best told the story. The battle reports, the in-universe dispatches, the lore that made the setting feel alive.

**Two more are settled by the standings, no vote needed:**

| Award               | Decided by                                                               | Rewards                                            |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **League Champion** | Most points; see [[content/league-rules/index#tiebreakers\|tiebreakers]] | Winning the league                                 |
| **Wooden Spoon**    | Last in the final standings                                              | Showed up regardless of standings. Worn with pride |

Each award comes with something to take home. Trophy, pin, spoon, whatever it is, you'll get something to remember your achievement.

> The Wooden Spoon is the one place co-winners are fine. Everything else resolves to one winner.

## Voting

- Ballots are **pre-printed with every player's name and army**, and your own is struck off your sheet. No self-voting.
- Each of the three categories takes a **ranked pick** (1st, 2nd, 3rd choice). If the first-choice votes tie, the lower choices break it, so the night ends with a single winner per award and no run-offs.
- The **store owner or an employee tallies the ballots**. A neutral party, where no player sees the count.
