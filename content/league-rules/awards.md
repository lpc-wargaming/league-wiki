# Awards

Everything here sits beside the **League Champion**, which is won on points over in the [[content/league-rules/index#scoring|rules]]. The awards split three ways: one you build up phase by phase just by painting, three the players vote on at the finale, and two that fall out of the final standings.

## Painting Stickers & the Iron Hobbyist

Each phase, paint your army to that phase's size (**3 colours + based**, including the phase's new additions) and you earn a **sticker** — a collectable for your army case, your shelf, or wherever you hoard such things. It's the same trigger as the painting points in the [[content/league-rules/index#painting|rules]], so one finished army pays twice — but the two rewards are separate. The **points** are the points (tracked digitally). The **sticker** is just swag, worth nothing on the table.

What the sticker celebrates *does* build toward one award:

> **Iron Hobbyist** — paint every phase you're part of, and at the finale you take home the pin: the badge for going the distance. No vote, no tie, no debate. You either painted it or you didn't.

Because painting can be caught up any time before the finale, falling behind never locks you out — finish the work and the phase counts. And because a late joiner only owes the phases they were actually here for, *every phase you're part of* is an honest bar for everyone, whether you started in Phase 1 or Phase 4.

## Finale Awards

Handed out once, on finale night. Kept few on purpose, so the votes don't scatter and each award means something different.

**The players vote on three** — your peers, your choice:

| Award            | Rewards                          |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Best Painted** | Made your army men a work of art |
| **Most Improved**| How far your brush travelled     |
| **Remembrancer** | Brought your army to life        |

**Best Painted** and **Most Improved** reward different things on purpose — one is the best army *now*, the other is the biggest leap from where you started — so a newcomer can take home glory standing right next to a veteran. **Remembrancer** goes to whoever best told the story: the battle reports, the in-universe dispatches, the lore that made the setting feel alive.

**Two more are settled by the standings, no vote needed:**

| Award               | Decided by                                                                  | Rewards                                            |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **League Champion** | Most points — see [[content/league-rules/index#tiebreakers\|tiebreakers]]   | Winning the league                                 |
| **Wooden Spoon**    | Last in the final standings                                                 | Showed up regardless of standings. Worn with pride |

Each award comes with something to take home — trophy, pin, or spoon.

> The Wooden Spoon is the one place co-winners are fine — a shared spoon is funnier than a lonely one. Everything else resolves to one winner.

## Voting

- Ballots are **pre-printed with every player's name and army**, and your own is struck off your sheet — no self-voting.
- Each of the three categories takes a **ranked pick** (1st, 2nd, 3rd choice). If the first-choice votes tie, the lower choices break it, so the night ends with a single winner per award and no run-offs.
- The **store owner or an employee tallies the ballots** — a neutral hand, and no player sees the count.
