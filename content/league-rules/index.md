# League Rules

> [!warning] Draft
> 11th Edition rules are still rolling out. Points values and missions will be finalized once the core rulebook is in hand.

## The Goal

No better way to kick off the new edition than with an escalation league. We want something casual that is easy for new players to have fun and learn the game and get an army painted along the way.

You don't need to start a brand new army to participate. Many of us have a backlog we want to finish and/or can't afford a new army. We don't want to penalize that.

However, it is against the spirit of the thing to come in with your fully painted 2000pt army. Let's use this opportunity to get something new on the table!

## Format

- **Phases:** 5 monthly phases, 500→750→1000→1500→2000 points. Phase 2/3 have a smaller increase for a more relaxed start.
- **Pace:** about one game every other week — roughly two league games per phase.
- **Lists:** rebuild between phases, but units you've already fielded are encouraged to stay — your army is growing, not restarting.
- **Army:** Can be a previous army, but the hobby is the point. If you want to repaint an old army to your current skill level, that's fine, but make sure you're getting fresh paint on. Aim for 1 new unit for your army per phase minimum.
- **Rules:** 11th Edition matched play, missions from the current Chapter Approved deck.
- **2000-point tournament** caps the league — a separate, open event for everyone. See [[schedule|Schedule]].

## Scoring

Points accumulate across the whole season. **Most points at the finale wins the league.**

**Up to two league games count each phase, and both count** — win, draw, or loss. No playing extra and keeping only your best; decide whether a game counts *before* you play it. Anything beyond your two is a friendly and doesn't score.

| Result                                               | Points |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Win                                                  | 3      |
| Draw                                                 | 2      |
| Loss                                                 | 1      |
| Army fully painted for the phase (3 colours + based) | +2     |
| Underdog (less league points than opponent)          | +1     |
| Rubberband (bottom 50% in the standings)             | +1     |

Painting bonus is earned **once per phase** and can be applied after your games, before the turnover to the next phase.

## Tiebreakers

Between players tied on total points, in order:

1. **Most phases fully painted** — this is a painting league first.
2. **Most wins** — more wins beats more draws.
3. **Championship match** — still level? The two play a decider at the finale.

## Catching up

You can join the league at any phase or even start over with a new army. The Underdog and Rubberband bonus points are designed to help catch you back up if you're behind. Furthermore, you start at the same points level of whoever is last in the standing. 

You'll lose out on any missed painting points from previous phases, but you can still earn them for the phase you're on. You get any stickers you missed so you're in the running for the Iron Hobbyist [[awards|Award]].

## Narrative

There are no narrative play rules, but you are highly encouraged to engage in the storytelling aspect of the game. We will be collaboratively fleshing out a lore-wiki for the bespoke setting. Narrative battle reports and world expanding suggestions are highly encouraged. Even reporting a few standout moments from your game is something.

Uncomfortable or uninterested? It won't be worth any points so it's no big deal. However, there will be an [[awards|Award]] related to whoever best makes their army come alive.
## Awards

Multiple awards including, Best Painted, Most Improved, the Wooden Spoon, and the per-phase painting stickers all live on the [[awards|Awards]] page. League Champion is decided here, on points.

## The Warzone

Every league game is also a battle in **[[warzone|The Warzone]]**, a narrative territory campaign for the setting. You report a result once and it counts twice — toward your league standing *and* your faction's hold on the map. The Warzone is its own contest with its own prize, separate from the League Champion, and a new player's faction can enter the story at any point, so nobody is shut out for joining late.

> [!note] In progress
> Full Warzone rules are still being finalized — see [[warzone|the Warzone page]] for the working draft.

## Sportsmanship

This league exists to get people playing. If there's no veteran to ask for rules disputes, roll a d6, move on, look it up after the game. Slow-play, gotcha rules lawyering, and netlist-stomping new players are all worth less than being someone people want to play again.

## New Players

New? Just let us know — this is the perfect time to start. Want a learning or demo game? Someone will be happy to walk you through it. Many players have more than one army, so you don't even need to have models!
