# The Warzone

> [!warning] Working draft
> This is the base concept, to be fleshed out in a refining session. The mechanics below are a starting point, not final.

> [!note] What this is
> The Warzone is the league's **narrative campaign** — a season-long fight over a map of the setting. Every league game is also a battle here. It runs *alongside* the [[content/league-rules/index|league]]: the league rewards **you** (league points and standing), the Warzone rewards your **faction** (territory, and the story). It's the same reported result feeding two systems, so it adds no rules to your games.

> The Warzone is one *kind* of campaign. If it endures, we can run other narrative campaigns under the same roof later — this is just the first.
